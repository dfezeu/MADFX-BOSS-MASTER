## Pro Tips for LinkedIn Outreach

1. **Always personalize** — Reference something specific about the person or company
2. **Keep it short** — Recruiters receive hundreds of messages
3. **Include key info quickly** — Company names, role types, relevant achievements
4. **Be professional but friendly** — Tone matters
5. **Follow up** — If no response in 5-7 days, send a polite follow-up
6. **Connect strategically** — Target recruiters who work at companies you're interested in

## How to Find Recruiters on LinkedIn

1. Search: `[Company] recruiter` or `recruiter at [Company]`
2. Use LinkedIn Jobs page → click "See all recruiters" on company pages
3. Search: `remote customer success recruiter` or similar
4. Join LinkedIn groups for your industry
5. Look for "Talent" or "Recruiting" in profile headlines
