## SALARY NEGOTIATION TIPS

When you get an offer:

1. **Don't accept immediately** — Say "This is great, can I take 24 hours to review?"
2. **Know your range** — Customer Success: $80K-110K | Operations: $55K-75K | AE: $70K-100K + commission
3. **Highlight your value** — "At Evo, I managed $2M portfolios and grew them to enterprise level"
4. **Negotiate if needed** — "I was hoping for $90K based on my experience. Is there flexibility?"
